#### Save a report

A saved report keeps the time range, granularity, filters, and grouping, so that you can open the same view again later or share it with your team.

1. Keep the report of chapter 2 (monthly, grouped by Region) and choose **Save to report library** at the top of the page:
    - (1) Enter the name `fcj-monthly-cost-by-region`.
    - (2) Choose **Save report**.

    ![Save to report library](/images/3/3-1.png)

#### Open the report library

2. In the navigation pane, choose **Cost Explorer Saved Reports** (1):
    - (2) The reports with a lock icon are **default reports** created by AWS, such as *Monthly costs by service*, *Daily costs*, *RI Utilization*, or the Savings Plans *Coverage report*. You can open and duplicate them, but not change or delete them.
    - (3) Your report `fcj-monthly-cost-by-region` is at the end of the list.

    ![Saved reports](/images/3/3-2.png)

#### Delete the report

3. Select `fcj-monthly-cost-by-region` (1), choose **Delete**, then confirm with **Delete** (2).

    ![Delete the report](/images/3/3-3.png)

{{% notice tip %}}
To receive a report regularly by email instead of opening the console, use **AWS Budgets** reports or **Cost Anomaly Detection** alerts in the same console.
{{% /notice %}}
