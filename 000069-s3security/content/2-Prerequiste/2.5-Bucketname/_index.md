#### Find the bucket name

1. Open the [S3 console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/s3/home) and search for `sid-security`.
2. Choose the bucket whose name matches the **BucketName** output of the stack. You will edit its **Permissions** in the next sections.

![Lab bucket](/images/2-prerequisite/2.5-bucket.png)

{{% notice warning %}}
In every policy below, replace `<your-bucket-name>` with your own bucket name.
{{% /notice %}}
