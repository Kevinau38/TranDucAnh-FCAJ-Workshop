#### What is a Savings Plan?

A **Savings Plan** is a pricing model: you commit to spend a fixed amount **per hour** (measured in USD/hour) for **1 or 3 years**. In return, the usage covered by the plan is billed at lower Savings Plans rates instead of On-Demand rates.

- Usage up to your commitment is billed at the Savings Plans rate.
- Usage above your commitment is billed at the normal On-Demand rate.
- If you use less than your commitment in an hour, you still pay the full commitment for that hour.

You do not choose instances or resources when you buy a plan. AWS applies the plan automatically to eligible usage in the account, and, with consolidated billing in AWS Organizations, to the other accounts of the organization.

#### Payment options

| Option | You pay | Discount |
|---|---|---|
| **All Upfront** | The whole term at purchase | Highest |
| **Partial Upfront** | At least half at purchase, the rest monthly | Medium |
| **No Upfront** | Everything monthly | Lowest |
